The buying group for independent wholesalers

Our network of leading independent wholesalers makes us the UK’s largest independent buying group. Owned by its members, Unitas delivers profitable growth for all stakeholders through its unrivalled buying power, and the services of its Support Centre.

Wholesaler

We harness the collective scale and strength of the group to negotiate the strongest trading terms, enhanced benefits and the most competitive promotions for our wholesale members.

Retailer

Our award-winning operation gives retailers access to the strongest promotions and a business boosting toolkit, allowing them to promote leading brands at competitive prices and thrive against the competition.

Supplier

We work closely with our supplier partners to develop long-term business relationships. We give them the tools and networking opportunities to create an effective and sustainable route to market across our membership.

Wholesaler Search

Together our wholesalers operate a nationwide cash & carry and delivered service that covers all key industry sectors including retail, foodservice, on-trade, specialist and export.
